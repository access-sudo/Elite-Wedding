The Timeline

How a wedding
is made

Four movements, eighteen months, one director. Nothing on the day is improvised because nothing on the day is new.

18 months, start to forever
I

First Dream

We sit with you before anything is booked. Six months of references, family stories and half-formed ideas become a single written brief.

II

The Sketch

Our design room draws the day by hand — floor plans, sightlines, the light at four in the afternoon. You approve a drawing, not a mood board.

III

The Season

Floral, kitchen, sound and styling teams rehearse on site. Every vendor is ours, briefed from the same drawing, in the same room.

IV

Forever

On the day, one director holds the timeline. You are handed a glass and nothing else to carry. Everything after that is yours to enjoy.
